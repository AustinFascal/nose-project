# User Persona: Bambang Hartono (The Cautious Target)

## "I don't trust easily, but scammers are getting too clever to ignore."

| Profile Details | Information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Name** | Bambang Hartono |
| **Age** | 45 Years Old |
| **Location** | Jakarta, Indonesia |
| **Occupation** | Senior Administrative Officer / Civil Servant |
| **Tech Savviness** | Moderate - Uses banking apps, WhatsApp, and email for work. |

---

### 👤 Background
Bambang is a seasoned professional who values security and order. He has seen many of his colleagues lose significant amounts of money to "investment schemes" and "official-looking" tax scams. Because of this, he has developed a healthy skepticism and rarely clicks on unknown links. However, he is aware that as scammers start using AI and sophisticated social engineering, even his caution might not be enough.

### 🎯 Goals & Motivations
*   **Asset Protection**: His primary goal is to ensure his hard-earned savings and pension fund remain untouched by criminals.
*   **Zero-Risk Digital Life**: He wants to use digital services (mobile banking, e-government) without the constant anxiety of being "hacked" or "tricked."
*   **Privacy Maintenance**: He is protective of his personal data and NIK (National ID Number).

### 🔴 Pain Points & Frustrations
*   **Sophisticated Deception**: Frustrated by how realistic fake bank websites and official letters have become.
*   **Cognitive Overload**: The effort required to verify every single message is exhausting.
*   **Fear of a "Momentary Lapse"**: He knows that he only needs to be distracted or tired for one second to make a mistake that could cost him everything.
*   **Unclear Recovery Paths**: He doesn't know exactly what to do in the first 5 minutes after realizing he might have been scammed.

### 🛠 Mengapa Dia Butuh Fitur Panic Button?
Meskipun Bambang adalah orang yang sangat berhati-hati dan tidak mudah percaya, fitur **Panic Button** di Nosè menjadi sangat krusial baginya karena beberapa alasan:

1.  **The "Safety Net" (Jaring Pengaman)**: Bambang menganggap Panic Button sebagai "rem darurat." Jika dia terlanjur berinteraksi dengan penipu (misalnya, memberikan kode OTP atau menginstal file .APK palsu) dan kemudian menyadarinya, dia butuh satu tombol yang bisa langsung menghentikan proses atau memberi panduan mitigasi instan.
2.  **Overcoming "Freeze" Response**: Saat seseorang menyadari mereka sedang ditipu, mereka sering mengalami kepanikan (freeze). Panic Button menghilangkan kebingungan dengan memberikan langkah-langkah terstruktur (seperti blokir rekening atau lapor bank) dalam hitungan detik.
3.  **Real-Time Intervention**: Jika dia merasa ada yang tidak beres saat sedang menelepon seseorang yang mengaku dari otoritas resmi, menekan Panic Button memungkinkannya untuk segera mendapatkan bantuan atau verifikasi tanpa harus menutup telepon terlebih dahulu.
4.  **Peace of Mind**: Mengetahui bahwa ada "tombol darurat" di ponselnya membuat Bambang merasa lebih percaya diri saat harus melakukan transaksi digital yang mendesak.

---

### 📱 Typical User Journey
1.  **Suspicion**: Bambang menerima telepon dari "kantor pajak" yang meminta verifikasi data mendesak lewat sebuah link.
2.  **Interaction**: Karena sedang sibuk dan tertekan, dia mengklik link tersebut dan memasukkan beberapa data sebelum tersadar ada yang aneh dengan URL-nya.
3.  **Panic**: Detak jantungnya meningkat. Dia sadar dia baru saja melakukan kesalahan fatal.
4.  **Response**: Alih-alih bingung mencari nomor telepon bank di Google, dia langsung menekan **Floating Panic Button** dari aplikasi Nosè.
5.  **Resolution**: Aplikasi segera memandu Bambang untuk mengamankan akunnya, mengambil screenshot bukti percakapan secara otomatis, dan memberikan kontak darurat yang valid.
