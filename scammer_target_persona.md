# User Persona: Bambang Hartono (The Cautious Target)

## "I don't trust easily, but scammers are getting too clever for caution alone to be enough."

| Profile Details | Information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Name** | Bambang Hartono |
| **Age** | 45 Years Old |
| **Location** | Jakarta, Indonesia |
| **Occupation** | Senior Administrative Officer / Civil Servant |
| **Tech Savviness** | Moderate — Comfortable with banking apps, WhatsApp, and official e-government portals. |

---

### 🌟 Lifestyle and Values
Bambang values stability, integrity, and the security of his family above all else. He works within a structured government environment in Jakarta but stays deeply connected to his local community and extended family through social and religious groups. His lifestyle is a balanced blend of disciplined professional hours followed by dedicated downtime spent with his wife and children. He is a mindful consumer who avoids flashy trends, preferring reliable brands and services that align with his values of simplicity, functionality, and long-term trust.

### 💼 Professional Context
Bambang manages all aspects of his administrative duties, from processing internal proposals and official correspondence to the final delivery of departmental reports. He primarily uses official government portals and secure internal networks for his work, communicating with colleagues through secure email and WhatsApp groups. He relies heavily on official circulars and his professional network of senior colleagues to stay informed about policy changes and security protocols.

---

### 👤 Background
Bambang is a seasoned professional who values security, order, and family stability. Having witnessed colleagues lose life savings to "official" tax scams and sophisticated "investment" schemes, he has developed a deep-seated skepticism toward unsolicited digital communication. He rarely clicks unknown links and manually verifies large transactions. However, he is increasingly anxious that AI-driven deepfakes and high-fidelity social engineering will eventually outpace his ability to detect them manually.

### 🧠 Personality Traits
*   **Highly Skeptical**: Operates on a "verify first, trust never" basis.
*   **Risk-Averse**: Prioritizes the safety of his pension and family assets over high-reward opportunities.
*   **Methodical**: Prefers standard procedures and clear instructions.
*   **Community-Minded**: Often warns his extended family and junior colleagues about potential digital threats.

### 📅 Daily Routine Description
Bambang begins his day by reviewing official memos and administrative tasks at the office. He uses WhatsApp extensively for both work and family coordination, often encountering "forwarded" messages that raise his suspicion. During his lunch break, he might check his mobile banking or pay utility bills via e-wallet. His evenings are spent with his family, where he often finds himself helping his elderly parents or teenage children identify suspicious messages. He values his downtime and finds the constant vigilance required to stay safe online to be a significant mental drain.

### 🛒 Decision-Making Patterns
When considering a new security tool like Nosè, Bambang relies heavily on the recommendations of trusted peers and official endorsements from government or banking institutions. He is not swayed by flashy marketing; instead, he looks for clear evidence of effectiveness and simple, non-technical explanations of how the technology works. He values "free-to-try" models or government-supported programs that allow him to test the tool's reliability before fully integrating it into his daily life.

### 💻 Tech Proficiency
*   **Business Tools**: Highly proficient with spreadsheets and administrative software.
*   **Communication**: Highly proficient with WhatsApp and secure email.
*   **Financial Tech**: Moderately proficient with mobile banking and QRIS payments.
*   **Cybersecurity**: Basic understanding of passwords and 2FA, but feels overwhelmed by AI-level threats.

### 🎯 Short-Term Goals
*   Protect his current savings and upcoming quarterly bonuses from potential digital theft.
*   Find a tool that can "second-guess" suspicious calls or messages when he is tired or distracted.

### 🏆 Long-Term Goals
*   Ensure a secure and untouched pension fund for his retirement.
*   Build a "digital shield" for his family so he doesn't have to worry about his parents or children falling for scams.

### 🚀 Key Drivers and Priorities
*   **Financial Stability**: Protecting the hard-earned assets that support his family's future.
*   **Mental Peace**: Reducing the "constant alert" state he feels whenever his phone rings.
*   **Civic Duty**: Being a reliable "Guardian" for his community.

### ⚠️ Practical Challenges
*   **Verification Time**: Manually checking every bank message or "government" notification takes too long.
*   **Complexity of Scams**: Scammers now use AI voices and professional-looking spoofed websites that look 99% identical to the real ones.

### 😫 Emotional Frustrations
*   **Hyper-Vigilance Fatigue**: The constant stress of having to be "perfect" in every digital interaction.
*   **Fear of Failure**: The heavy burden of knowing a single mistake could wipe out years of work.
*   **Isolation**: Feeling like the "police officer" of the family group chat, often being the only one calling out scams.

### 🚧 Obstacles to Success
*   **Cognitive Load**: Distractions at work or exhaustion at the end of the day make him vulnerable to "momentary lapses" in judgment.
*   **Lack of Immediate Tools**: Most security software is too technical or doesn't address the social/emotional manipulation of scams.

### 🗺️ The Journey to Nosè

#### Awareness Stage
Bambang first hears about Nosè through a "Digital Literacy" seminar at his workplace or a recommendation from a tech-savvy colleague who is already a "Trusted Guardian." He might also see a public service announcement on an official government social media channel.

#### Consideration Stage
He visits the Nosè website, looking specifically for "How it works" and "Who are the Guardians?" He compares Nosè to generic antivirus software and realizes that Nosè focuses on the *human* side of scams (calls, WhatsApp messages) rather than just files and viruses. He looks for testimonials from other civil servants or professionals in his age group.

#### Decision Triggers
The final trigger is a "close call"—a very convincing WhatsApp message from a "bank officer" that he almost believed. The realization that he is no longer safe by caution alone, combined with a recommendation from a trusted friend, leads him to install the app.

#### Post-Purchase Behavior
After installation, he immediately feels a "weight lifted" from his shoulders. He uses the "Scan Screenshot" feature for every suspicious message and feels reassured by the real-time feedback. He quickly becomes an advocate, telling his family group chat: "I'm using Nosè now, it's like having a professional security guard on my phone."

#### Loyalty Drivers
His loyalty is maintained by the app's consistent reliability and the "human touch" of the Guardian community. He values updates that explain new scam trends in simple language and a user interface that stays clean and intuitive as he ages.

### 👁️ View of Competitors
*   **Established Antivirus**: Views them as bloated, battery-draining, and too focused on technical "viruses" rather than the "scams" he actually faces.
*   **Bank Alert Systems**: Finds them too slow and reactive—they often tell you *after* the money is gone.

### ✨ Desired Brand Experience
Bambang wants Nosè to feel like a **Trusted Partner**—a tool that is powerful and sophisticated under the hood, but simple, calm, and supportive on the surface. He wants a brand that respects his privacy, understands his local context (Indonesia/ASEAN), and empowers him to be a hero for his family without needing a computer science degree.
