# User Persona: Nur Cholisah

## "Protecting my community starts with my own digital literacy."

| Profile Details | Information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Name** | Nur Cholisah |
| **Age** | 22 Years Old |
| **Location** | Surabaya, Indonesia |
| **Occupation** | 4th Year Computer Science Student at Universitas Airlangga (UNAIR) |
| **Tech Savviness** | High - Digital native, active on social media and WhatsApp |

---

### 👤 Background
Nur is a motivated final-year student living in the bustling city of Surabaya. She is active in campus life and frequently visits the **American Corner (Amcor) at UNAIR**. While she is technically proficient, she is increasingly worried about the rising tide of digital scams in Indonesia, particularly those targeting her less tech-savvy family members and younger peers.

### 🎯 Goals & Motivations
*   **Family Security**: To protect her parents and younger siblings from the financial and psychological impact of phishing and social engineering.
*   **Community Leadership**: To become a "Trusted Guardian" within her social circles and use her knowledge to help others.
*   **Professional Growth**: To gain certification in digital security and literacy, enhancing her resume and practical skills.
*   **Proactive Defense**: To have a reliable way to report and react to suspicious digital activity instantly.

### 🔴 Pain Points & Frustrations
*   **Constant Scam Attempts**: Receives unsolicited WhatsApp messages daily, ranging from fake job offers to "parcel delivery" notifications.
*   **Lack of Official Reporting**: Feels that reporting scams through traditional channels is slow and often yields no results.
*   **Misinformation**: Frustrated by how quickly "hoax" news and scam links spread in family WhatsApp groups.
*   **Fear of the Unknown**: Worried about the evolution of AI-driven scams (deepfakes, voice cloning) that are harder to detect.

### 🛠 Why Nosè (Node Safe) is Perfect for Her
*   **The Panic Button**: Provides her with an "immediate action" tool if she or someone she's with falls victim to an active scam.
*   **Education Hub**: She can follow the Day 1-3 modules at her own pace, reinforcing her existing knowledge with ASEAN-specific case studies.
*   **Trusted Guardian Certification**: The e-KYC and final simulation give her a sense of achievement and a verifiable "badge of trust" in her community.
*   **Workshop Integration**: Allows her to easily find and register for physical events at Amcor UNAIR, bridging the gap between digital and physical community support.

---

### 📱 Typical User Journey
1.  **Discovery**: Nur sees a poster for a Nosè workshop at Amcor UNAIR.
2.  **Onboarding**: She downloads the app and completes the Day 1 module on social engineering during her commute.
3.  **Action**: She triggers the "Panic Button" as a test or when she encounters a suspicious APK file sent to her.
4.  **Growth**: She completes the 3-day program, passes the simulation, and successfully earns her **Trusted Guardian Badge**.
5.  **Impact**: She starts using the app to verify links for her parents before they click them.
